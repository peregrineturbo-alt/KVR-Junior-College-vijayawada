## About Us
KVR Junior College was founded in 1978 by KVR Sir, a Physics teacher with more than 48 years of classroom experience. The college is located in Labbipet, Vijayawada, and focuses on building strong conceptual foundations in MPC and BiPC students. It combines a structured study plan for the Intermediate board exams with preparation for entrance exams such as IIT-JEE and NEET. Board and entrance preparation run in one timetable, not as separate tracks.

## Programs Offered

All programs run for two years (Intermediate first and second year).

### Science streams
- **MPC Intermediate** – Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry. Focus on board exam preparation and engineering foundations (board exams and AP EAPCET).
- **BiPC Intermediate** – Biology, Physics and Chemistry. For students aiming at medical, pharmacy and life science careers (board exams and NEET).
- **IIT-JEE Integrated** – MPC Intermediate combined with structured JEE Main and JEE Advanced preparation.
- **NEET Integrated** – BiPC Intermediate with dedicated NEET UG preparation. NCERT-focused, with a weekly test series.
- **MPC (Board Focus)** and **BiPC (Board Focus)** – tracks centred on the Intermediate board exams.

### Commerce and humanities streams
- **MEC** – Mathematics, Economics and Commerce. For students interested in CA, CS, business and finance careers.
- **CEC** – Civics, Economics and Commerce. For students exploring civil services, law and humanities careers.

## Teaching Approach

1. **Concept-first teaching** – Every topic is built from fundamentals so students understand the reasoning, not only the method.
2. **Board and entrance together** – An integrated plan lets students prepare for Intermediate boards and IIT-JEE or NEET without managing two separate schedules.
3. **Regular testing** – Weekly topic tests, monthly full-length exams and performance analysis to find gaps early.
4. **Mentorship** – Each student is assigned a mentor who tracks academics, attendance and wellbeing across the two years.
5. **NCERT-centric approach** – Especially for NEET, NCERT is treated as the primary text, with line-by-line reading and practice from NCERT examples.
6. **Parent communication** – Regular parent-teacher meetings and progress reports.
7. **Small batches and doubt clearing** – The college describes its batches as small, with doubt-clearing sessions and one-to-one mentoring.

## Entrance Exam Facts (as of November 2026)

These facts come from public sources and can change. Confirm them with the official bodies before making decisions.

- **JEE Main** is conducted by NTA (jeemain.nta.nic.in). Candidates must have passed Class 12 or be appearing in it. Physics and Mathematics are compulsory. Public summaries report two sessions a year and up to six attempts across three consecutive years.
- **NEET UG** is conducted by NTA, and the syllabus is published through NMC (nmc.org.in). The paper has 180 questions for 720 marks: 45 Physics, 45 Chemistry and 90 Biology. Eligibility needs Physics, Chemistry, Biology or Biotechnology, and English. Mathematics is not required.
- **AP EAPCET** is conducted by APSCHE (cets.apsche.ap.gov.in). The 2026 instruction booklet sets the rank as 75% entrance score and 25% Intermediate group marks, with a minimum of 45% in group subjects (40% for reserved categories).
- Students who finish Class 10 in 2027 will write Intermediate second-year exams and these entrance exams in 2029. Rules for that year will be published by the exam bodies.

**Q: What is the difference between MPC and BiPC?**
A: MPC covers Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry and leads to engineering entrances (JEE Main, JEE Advanced, AP EAPCET Engineering). BiPC covers Biology, Physics and Chemistry and leads to NEET UG for medical courses, and to AP EAPCET for pharmacy and agriculture.

**Q: Do Intermediate marks matter for entrance ranks?**
A: For AP EAPCET, yes. APSCHE's 2026 booklet gives 25% weight to Intermediate group marks. JEE Main and NEET ranks come from the exam score, but both have Intermediate eligibility minimums. Confirm the current rules with the official bodies.

**Q: Is Mathematics needed for NEET?**
A: No. NEET needs Physics, Chemistry, Biology or Biotechnology, and English.

**Q: Can a Class 11 student apply for JEE Main?**
A: No. Candidates must have passed Class 12 or be appearing in it.

**Q: Is NCERT enough for NEET?**
A: NCERT is the base text, because the NEET syllabus follows it. KVR Junior College teaches NCERT line by line and adds question practice. Biology carries 90 of the 180 questions.
